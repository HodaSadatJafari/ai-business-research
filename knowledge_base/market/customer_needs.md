# Customer Support Analytics Research

## Interview Findings
In a fictional set of 18 interviews with support managers, 12 wanted a clearer way to identify recurring product bugs, 9 mentioned slow reporting, and 7 wanted to connect ticket trends to product roadmap decisions. These counts are illustrative project data, not a market-wide estimate.

## Buying Criteria
Interviewees prioritized integration with existing ticket systems, transparent topic labels, and predictable pricing. Several managers said they would not trust fully automatic topic labels without a way to inspect source tickets.
