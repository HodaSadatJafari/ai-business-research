# SignalDesk Product

## Features
SignalDesk imports support tickets from HelpHarbor and MailDock. It groups tickets by topic, shows weekly trend charts, and lets analysts export a CSV of tagged tickets. A manager can review individual tickets behind each trend.

## Limits
SignalDesk refreshes imported tickets every six hours. It does not ingest phone transcripts or social media messages. Topic labels require human review before they are shared externally.
