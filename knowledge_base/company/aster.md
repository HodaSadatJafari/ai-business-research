# Aster Analytics

## Positioning
Aster Analytics sells SignalDesk, a customer support analytics platform for mid-sized subscription businesses. The company positions SignalDesk as a tool for support managers who need to find recurring customer problems and prioritize fixes. It does not sell a customer-facing chatbot.

## Pricing
SignalDesk Team costs $480 per month for up to 20 support agents. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and adds single sign-on and a dedicated data retention agreement.
